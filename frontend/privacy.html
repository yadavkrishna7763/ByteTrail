Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 24, 2026 | Effective Date: August 24, 2026

1. Overview & Mission

ByteTrail is an enterprise-grade Security Operations Center (SOC) threat intelligence and automated forensic analysis platform (Smart India Hackathon Problem Statement 26106). We are dedicated to safeguarding organizations from sophisticated phishing campaigns, business email compromise (BEC), spoofing, and malicious attachments while strictly protecting user privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how ByteTrail collects, analyzes, processes, and protects information when you connect your email accounts (via Google OAuth 2.0 or IMAP) or ingest RFC 822 email files.

2. Google OAuth 2.0 & Restricted Scopes Disclosure

Google API Limited Use Disclosure: ByteTrail's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Permissions Requested

When you authenticate with Google, ByteTrail requests the following permissions strictly for cybersecurity scanning:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly: Grants read-only access to email headers and message contents strictly to scan for phishing lures, malicious links, typosquatting domains, and SPF/DKIM authentication failures.
  • openid / userinfo.email / userinfo.profile: Used exclusively to identify the connected mailbox and display your authorized account name on your private SOC dashboard.

Restricted Usage Guarantees:

  • ByteTrail NEVER modifies, sends, or deletes your emails.
  • ByteTrail NEVER sells, rents, or shares your email content with third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.
  • ByteTrail NEVER uses email content to train generalized artificial intelligence models without explicit organizational consent.
  • Human analysts do not view your raw emails unless specifically flagged as high-risk threat indicators during an authorized organizational incident investigation.

3. Information We Collect & Analyze

ByteTrail extracts specific technical artifacts to execute our 4-vector cybersecurity intelligence pipeline:

  • Header Metadata: Received relay IP hops, Message-ID, DKIM cryptographic signatures, SPF authentication results, and DMARC alignment records.
  • Sender & Routing Data: Return-Path, Envelope-From, Display Name, and reply routing addresses to detect CEO fraud and display name spoofing.
  • Threat Indicators (IoCs): Embedded URLs, domain names, file attachment names, MIME types, and cryptographic hashes (SHA-256).
  • Plaintext Message Body: Processed by our localized NLP Heuristic Engine to calculate phishing, urgency, and financial threat scores.

4. Data Storage & Security Measures

ByteTrail adheres to strict defense-in-depth security standards:

  • Cryptographic Evidence Chain of Custody: Every analyzed email is hashed using standard SHA-256 to guarantee forensic non-repudiation and evidence integrity.
  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmitted between your browser, Vercel frontend, Render backend, and TiDB Cloud database is encrypted using TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.
  • Encryption at Rest: Databases utilize enterprise AES-256 encryption at rest.
  • Zero-Retention Controls: You have full control to disconnect any connected mailbox at any time, which terminates background polling immediately.

5. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have full autonomy over your data:

  • 1-Click Disconnect: Navigate to the Live Mailboxes tab on your dashboard and click the disconnect icon. This revokes the active token and halts all ingestion immediately.
  • Google Permissions Revocation: You can revoke ByteTrail's access at any time directly through your Google Account Security Settings.
  • Complete Data Erasure Request: To request complete deletion of your forensic logs and ingested email hashes from the database, contact our privacy officer at krishnayadav770694@gmail.com.
