The 4-Vector Cybersecurity Analysis Engine

Every inbound email payload traverses our synchronized four-tier threat correlation pipeline.

Vector 1

NLP Phishing & Fraud Heuristics

Evaluates psychological urgency triggers, credential harvesting lures, lookalike typosquat domains, and CEO BEC wire fraud indicators using fine-tuned NLP heuristics.

Vector 2

RFC 822 Header & Signature Forensics

Validates SPF records, DKIM digital signatures, and DMARC alignment. Detects domain spoofing and From vs. Reply-To sender mismatches.

Vector 3

Origin GeoIP & Threat Intel

Traces mail relay hops to physical countries, autonomous system networks (ASN), Tor exit relays, VPN anonymizers, and attributed APT threat actor groups.

Vector 4

Composite Risk Index & Scoring

Aggregates multi-vector threat signals into a calibrated 0.0 to 100.0 risk score, categorizing incidents into Benign (Low), Suspicious (Medium), or Critical (High).
